Overview

Drawing on project-level data from 2014-2023, the book examines the institutional and fiscal dynamics of China's public-private partnership development. It considers national policy, local implementation, subnational diffusion, state-owned enterprise participation, financing incentives, and fiscal constraints.

The empirical analysis uses spatial diffusion theory, transaction cost theory, capital structure theory, and cutback management theory to interpret China's PPP experience and its implications for governance and public policy.
