The story behind the market

A decade of PPP expansion, adjustment, and reform

Drawing on a detailed project-level dataset covering 2014-2023, the book explains the institutional logic behind China's PPP trajectory. It connects the evolution of national policy with local implementation, regional diffusion, state-owned enterprise participation, financing incentives, and fiscal control.

Rather than treating PPPs only as infrastructure contracts, the book places them within the wider political, fiscal, and intergovernmental system that shapes how public projects are selected, financed, and governed.
