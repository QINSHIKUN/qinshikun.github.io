Research portfolio

Research

My research examines how governments design, finance, and manage public projects and procurement systems. I focus on public-private partnerships (PPPs), government procurement, local public finance, and government-enterprise relations in China.

Research themes

Government procurement

Digital transformation, policy performance, contract management, and public-sector capability.

Public-private partnerships

Private participation, spatial diffusion, policy uncertainty, governance, and project performance.

Local public finance

Fiscal stress, financing strategies, fiscal rules, welfare expenditure, and local government behavior.
