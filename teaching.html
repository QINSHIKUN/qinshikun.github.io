Teaching approach

I encourage students to move from a clearly defined public problem to an explicit theory, an appropriate research design, careful interpretation, and feasible policy implications. Cases from public procurement, PPP governance, and local public finance help connect analytical concepts to real administrative choices.

Problem first

Begin with a concrete governance or policy question.

Evidence aware

Match claims to data, methods, and credible identification.

Decision oriented

Translate findings into precise, usable policy implications.
